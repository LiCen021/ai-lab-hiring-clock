How categories are assigned

Rules are checked top to bottom against the job title (case-insensitive); each rule matches any of its keywords (regular expressions, \b = word boundary). The first rule that matches decides the category; titles matching none go to Other. Earlier rules exist to catch titles that would otherwise be misfiled, e.g. “User Experience Researcher” is Design, not Research.
